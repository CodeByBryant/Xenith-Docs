# The Interface

Xenith is built to stay out of your way. Once you know the few core surfaces,
you can move anywhere in a couple of keystrokes.

## The dashboard

Your home screen (**/app**) is a personalized dashboard. It opens with a
greeting, today's date and a plain line of facts ("1 of 3 intentions done, 25
minutes focused today"), then surfaces what's relevant right now:

- **Today:** your [intentions](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md) with
  progress, the next thing to [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) on, and anything
  still open from earlier days. With nothing planned yet, **Draft my day** lists what
  is already in Xenith, still-open intentions and today's calendar events, each with
  where it came from. You tick what you want and press **Add** (nothing is added
  before that).
- **From your tools:** tasks from [Todoist, Microsoft To Do or Google
  Tasks](../integrations/task-sources.md), if you connected them. Shown only when
  there is something to decide.
- **This week:** Focus minutes and intentions done for each day, plus calendar
  events. Counts only, no comparisons.
- **Calendar** and a **Balance** wheel of your [Life Dimensions](../life-dimensions/overview.md).
- Your active [routines](../daily-workflow/routines.md), your latest reflection and
  your projects.
- **Your tools:** the free dimension tools (calories, Finances, Books, Growth
  Path), ordered by the dimensions you chose. Pro tools show as a compact locked
  tile.
- A search bar in the header (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) and quick actions.

The dashboard is a calm overview, not a control tower. It shows direction, not
pressure.

### Arrange your dashboard

Press **Customize** at the top right of the dashboard to make it yours. It is free
for everyone, and the dashboard stays exactly as it was until you change something.

- **Reorder.** Drag a card by its handle, or use the **Move up** and **Move down**
  buttons (they work from the keyboard too). A card stays in its own column: *This
  week*, routines and reflection in the main column, *Calendar* and the rest in the
  side column.
- **Hide and add back.** Hide what you don't use. Hidden cards wait under **Hidden**,
  and you can add them back at any time.
- **Your tools.** The row of tools under the cards arranges the same way. Which tools
  you have still follows the dimensions you chose and your plan. Locked Pro tiles
  stay at the end and can't be moved.
- **Reset to default** puts everything back. Changes save as you make them.

*Today* and *From your tools* always stay at the top of the main column.

If you hide a page in [Settings](../account/settings.md#pages), its card leaves the
dashboard too. Showing the page again brings the card back where it was.

Five more cards start hidden. Add any of them from **Customize**:

- **Quick capture:** type a thought and it goes to your [Inbox](#the-inbox).
- **Water & supplements:** today's water with one-tap **+250 ml** and **+500 ml**, and
  your supplements to tick off.
- **Mood check:** one tap sets today's mood. It changes only the mood, so a
  [reflection](../daily-workflow/reflection.md) you already wrote today stays as it is.
- **Habits:** today's [habits](../daily-workflow/habits.md), ticked or counted in a tap.
- **Reach out:** the people you set a contact goal for whose time has come (see
  [Relationships](../life-dimensions/relationships.md); Connections is part of Pro).

## Sidebar navigation

The left sidebar is your map of the app: Dashboard, Intentions, Focus, Coach,
Reflection, Life Dimensions, Growth, Projects, Worlds, Inbox, Habits, Routines,
Calendar and Insights, in three groups (Daily, Growth and Other). Settings opens from
your profile button at the bottom.

- **Fold a group.** Press a group's name to fold it. The page you are on stays visible,
  and Xenith remembers what you folded on that device.
- **Hide pages you don't use.** [Settings](../account/settings.md#pages) can take
  Habits, Routines, Inbox, Calendar and Insights out of the menu.
- **Collapse the whole sidebar** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd>) when you want
  maximum room to think. In the narrow version every page stays one tap away.

## Command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on Mac)
anywhere in Xenith to open the **command palette**. Start typing to jump to any
section, project, or page by name, with no clicking through menus.

> **Tip**
>
> The command palette is the fastest way to navigate. If you only learn one
> shortcut, make it <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>.

## Quick capture

A thought shouldn't have to wait for the right screen. **Quick capture** lets you
jot something down instantly. It lands in your [Inbox](#the-inbox) so you can
sort it later without breaking your flow.

## The inbox

The **Inbox** is your holding area for anything captured on the fly: stray ideas,
tasks, links, things to revisit. Process it on your own schedule: turn items
into intentions, project notes, or reflections, or simply clear them.

## The calendar

The **Calendar** gives you a time-based view of your activity and plans, so you
can see your intentions, sessions, and notable entries in context across the
days and weeks.

## Light and dark themes

Xenith defaults to a dark theme designed for long, focused sessions. Toggle to
light from the header control at any time. Your choice is remembered per device.
