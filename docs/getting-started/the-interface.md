# The Interface

Xenith is built to stay out of your way. Once you know the few core surfaces,
you can move anywhere in a couple of keystrokes.

## The dashboard

Your home screen (**/app**) is a personalized dashboard. It opens with a
greeting, today's date and a plain line of facts ("1 of 3 intentions done, 25
minutes focused today"), then surfaces what's relevant right now:

- **Projects**, across the top: the pages you opened most recently, your favorites,
  and what is dated in the next week in your databases, with **New page** (or one from a
  template) to start something. It reads page titles only, and a date that has passed
  is shown as a date, not as something overdue.
- **Today:** your [intentions](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md) with
  progress, the next thing to [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) on, and anything
  still open from earlier days. With nothing planned yet, **Draft my day** lists what
  is already in Xenith, still-open intentions and today's calendar events, each with
  where it came from. You tick what you want and press **Add** (nothing is added
  before that).
- **From your tools:** tasks from [Todoist, Microsoft To Do or Google
  Tasks](../integrations/task-sources.md), if you connected them. Shown only when
  there is something to decide.
- **This week:** Focus minutes and intentions done for each day, plus calendar
  events. Counts only, no comparisons.
- **Calendar**.
- Your active [routines](../daily-workflow/routines.md) and your latest reflection.
- A **Balance** wheel of your [Life Dimensions](../life-dimensions/overview.md), your
  **World**, and **Your tools** (the free dimension tools, ordered by the dimensions you
  chose, with Pro tools as a compact locked tile). A dashboard you haven't arranged yet
  doesn't show these three: add them from **Customize**. If you have already arranged
  your dashboard, nothing is taken away.
- A search bar in the header (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) and quick actions.

The dashboard is a calm overview, not a control tower. It shows direction, not
pressure.

### Arrange your dashboard

Press **Customize** at the top right of the dashboard to make it yours. It is free
for everyone, and the dashboard stays exactly as it was until you change something.

- **Reorder.** Drag a card by its handle, or use the **Move up** and **Move down**
  buttons (they work from the keyboard too). A card stays in its own column: *This
  week*, routines and reflection in the main column, *Calendar* and the rest in the
  side column.
- **Hide and add back.** Hide what you don't use. Hidden cards wait under **Hidden**,
  and you can add them back at any time.
- **Your tools.** The row of tools under the cards arranges the same way. Which tools
  you have still follows the dimensions you chose and your plan. Locked Pro tiles
  stay at the end and can't be moved.
- **Reset to default** puts everything back. Changes save as you make them.

*Today* and *From your tools* always stay at the top of the main column.

Six more cards start hidden. Add any of them from **Customize**:

- **Quick capture:** type a thought and it goes to your [Inbox](#the-inbox).
- **Water & supplements:** today's water with one-tap **+250 ml** and **+500 ml**, and
  your supplements to tick off.
- **Mood check:** one tap sets today's mood. It changes only the mood, so a
  [reflection](../daily-workflow/reflection.md) you already wrote today stays as it is.
- **Habits:** today's [habits](../daily-workflow/habits.md), ticked or counted in a tap.
- **Projects list:** a short list of your projects. The **Projects** section across the
  top of the dashboard has taken its place, so it stays hidden unless you want both.
- **Reach out:** the people you set a contact goal for whose time has come (see
  [Relationships](../life-dimensions/relationships.md); Connections is part of Pro).

## Sidebar navigation

The left sidebar is ordered the way the app is used. **Dashboard** is on its own at the
top, then three groups:

- **Daily:** Plan, Calendar, Focus and Reflection.
- **Understand:** Insights, Life Dimensions, Growth Path and Coach.
- **Workspace:** Projects and Worlds.

Every page has its own icon, and Settings opens from your profile button at the bottom.
Nothing is folded away: all of it is one click from where you are.

**Collapse the sidebar completely** with the button at the left of the top bar, or
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> on Mac). It leaves no
strip behind, so the page gets the whole width. Press the same button or shortcut to
bring it back; Xenith remembers your choice on that device. On a phone the sidebar opens
from the menu button instead.

## Command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on Mac)
anywhere in Xenith to open the **command palette**. Start typing to jump to any
section, project, or page by name, with no clicking through menus.

> **Tip**
>
> The command palette is the fastest way to navigate. If you only learn one
> shortcut, make it <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>.

## Plan

**Plan** puts the things you do each day on one page, in four tabs:

- **Today** is for doing. From the top: a box to capture a thought, the day you are
  looking at (the arrows move both your intentions and your habits), your
  [intentions](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md), your
  [routines](../daily-workflow/routines.md) and your
  [habits](../daily-workflow/habits.md). Anything you finish turns green.
- **Routines** is where you build and edit your routines.
- **Habits** is where you add, edit and put away habits.
- **Inbox** is everything you captured that is waiting to be sorted. The tab shows how
  many are waiting.

A tab opens straight from its address: **/app/plan?tab=routines**, **habits** or
**inbox**. The addresses of the four pages Plan replaced (Intentions, Routines, Habits
and Inbox) still work: they open Plan.

## Quick capture

A thought shouldn't have to wait for the right screen. **Quick capture** lets you
jot something down instantly, from anywhere (press <kbd>Q</kbd>) or from the box at the
top of [Plan](#plan). It lands in your inbox so you can sort it later without breaking
your flow.

## The inbox

The **inbox** is your holding area for anything captured on the fly: stray ideas,
tasks, links, things to revisit. It is the **Inbox** tab on [Plan](#plan), with a count of what is waiting. Capture from the box on Plan and the confirmation has an **Open inbox** button. Process it on your own
schedule: turn items into intentions, or simply clear them.

## The calendar

The **Calendar** gives you a time-based view of your activity and plans, so you
can see your intentions, sessions, and events in context across the days and weeks.
It has month, week, day and Gantt views: see [Calendar](../daily-workflow/calendar.md).

## The Focus timer

While a [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) session is running, a small timer follows you
from page to page, so you can leave Focus to write a page or check a database and still
see the time. It has pause and resume, and a way back to Focus. It isn't shown on the
Focus page itself.

## Offline

Xenith opens without a connection and shows what your device has saved, read-only. A bar
under the top of the page tells you when you're offline. See
[Using Xenith offline](./offline.md).

## What's new

After an update, a card opens once with a picture and what changed. Close it and it
doesn't come back for that release, on any of your devices. If you were away for several
releases, you see the latest card only. The full history is in the
[Changelog](../changelog.md).

## Light and dark themes

Xenith follows your device: if it is set to dark, so is Xenith, and the same for light.
To choose one yourself, open **Settings** and set **Theme** under **Preferences** to
**Light**, **Dark** or **System**, then press **Save changes**. On a phone there is also a
theme button in the top bar. Your choice is remembered per device, so you can run dark on
your laptop and light on a bright office monitor.
