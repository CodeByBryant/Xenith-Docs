# Using Xenith offline

Xenith opens and reads without a connection. It's **read-only** while you're offline: you
can look at what you've opened on a device, and changes can be made again once you're
back online.

## What works

- **Every page of the app opens.** The first time you use Xenith on a device, it quietly
  keeps the app ready in the background a few seconds after the page settles. It skips
  this on a data-saver or very slow connection. After that, any page opens without a
  connection, including ones you have never visited.
- **What you've looked at is there.** The lists and numbers on the pages you opened
  recently stay on the device, so [Plan](./the-interface.md#plan), the
  [Calendar](../daily-workflow/calendar.md), [Insights](../growth-and-insights/insights.md)
  and the rest show what they showed the last time you had a connection.
- **Projects.** The pages and databases you opened open offline, with their text and
  rows. Pages and databases you have never opened on this device are not there.
- **Signed in.** You stay signed in while you're offline, even for longer than an hour.

## What waits

Anything that changes something needs a connection: adding or ticking an intention,
logging, editing a page or a row, starting a [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md)
session, importing, the Coach, syncing. Offline, a change is **refused when you make it**
and nothing is held back to send later, so what you see is always what is saved. Editors
are read-only. A bar under the top of the page tells you when you're offline.

A session that finishes while you're offline can't be recorded. Focus says so, so you
aren't left wondering.

A page you haven't opened on this device recently may look empty offline. That's because
there is nothing saved for it, not because anything was removed.

## When you're back online

The bar goes away, the pages you have open refresh, and you can make changes again. You
don't need to reload.

## What stays on your device

What's saved belongs to your account. The lists and numbers are kept for up to a week.
The pages and databases you opened are kept until newer ones you open take their place.
**Signing out removes all of it**, and so does signing in as a different person. It
works the same in the installed app (see **Get the app** in [Settings](../account/settings.md#get-the-app)).
