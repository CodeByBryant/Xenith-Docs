# Onboarding

The first time you sign in, Xenith asks for just enough to give you a useful
first day: a goal, one intention, a first look at your World, a short Focus
session if you want one, and (if you like) a quick sense of where you are in
each area of life. It takes a few minutes. Everything after the intention is
optional, and **Back** and **Forward** let you review or change anything before
you finish.

## The flow

1. **Your name and timezone.** Timezone matters. It's what every date and
   reminder in Xenith is based on.
2. **Pick one goal.** Seven options. Each one shows the three
   **[Life Dimensions](../life-dimensions/overview.md)** it keeps in view, so
   your goal decides which areas Xenith focuses on. Pick whichever fits today.
   If you arrived from one of our feature pages or articles, the matching goal is
   highlighted as a suggestion. Nothing is chosen for you.

   | Goal | Dimensions kept in view |
   | --- | --- |
   | Get organized | Work, Rest, Purpose |
   | Focus better | Work, Mind, Rest |
   | Improve my health | Health, Rest, Mind |
   | Build better routines | Health, Rest, Purpose |
   | Make progress on a project | Work, Learning, Purpose |
   | Feel more balanced | Relationships, Rest, Health |
   | Understand my patterns | Mind, Rest, Work |

3. **Set one intention.** A single, specific thing you want to move forward
   today. This is the heart of the deliberate-living loop. Under it you can
   choose **Email me around** an hour. It is off until you pick one, and Xenith
   then sends one email around that time. If that hour has already passed today,
   the intention is planned for tomorrow instead. See
   [Email reminders](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md#email-reminders).
4. **Create your World (optional).** Pick what your planet becomes
   (Terra, Ocean, Desert, Ice or Ember), roll a new one if you like, and give it
   a name. **Choose later** creates nothing, and **Turn Worlds off** removes the
   step and hides Worlds everywhere. See [Worlds](../growth-and-insights/worlds.md).
5. **Give it a few minutes (optional).** Run a 10, 15 or 25 minute
   [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) session on your intention, or choose
   **I'll do this later**. When it ends you come straight back to onboarding.
6. **How did that go? (only after a Focus session).** Say whether you made
   progress (Yes, A little or Not really), rate how focused you were, and note
   what got in the way if you like. If you answer Yes, you can mark your
   intention done from here. It is never ticked for you.
7. **Rate your dimensions (optional).** Drag a slider from 1 (very low) to 10
   (very high) for how each area is going right now. The three areas from your
   goal come first, and the other five are behind **Rate the other 5 areas**.
   A rough sense is enough. A slider counts as rated only once you touch it, so
   nothing is pre-filled, and only the areas you rate
   are saved, as your starting point under **Life Dimensions**. **Skip for now** moves on
   without saving anything.
8. **Connect your tools (optional).** A grid of the tools Xenith can work with:
   Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Docs, Notion, Microsoft To Do, Todoist and
   Apple Calendar. Open a card, switch on only what you want, and Xenith asks that
   app for just that. Google Docs and the Notion import switch connect an account
   for [importing](../projects-import/import-overview.md); nothing is imported during
   onboarding. Everything here is free, can be skipped, and never blocks finishing
   setup. See [Integrations](../integrations/calendar-sync.md).
9. **Your Xenith system.** A one-screen summary: your goal, the dimensions it
   keeps in view, anything you rated, and your intention. Then straight into
   the app.

Use **Back** and **Forward** at the top of any step to review or change
something. Going back keeps what you entered, and an edited intention updates
the one you already set instead of adding another. Your browser's Back button
works too. If you leave partway, Xenith picks up where you stopped.

## After onboarding

If you skipped the Focus step, your dashboard offers a short
[Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) session on your intention ("Give it 10
minutes?"). Start it, dismiss it, or leave it. It goes away on its own after 24
hours, and for good once you complete a session. If you finished onboarding
without a World, the dashboard offers to create one, once.

## Changing your setup later

Most of what you chose during onboarding can be changed later.

- **Ratings:** re-rate any of the eight dimensions any time under
  **Life Dimensions** in the sidebar.
- **Your goal and its dimensions:** these are set during onboarding. Every
  dimension stays available under **Life Dimensions** whichever goal you picked; the goal
  decides which tools your dashboard puts first.
- **Your World:** customize it, rename it or turn Worlds off any time. See [Worlds](../growth-and-insights/worlds.md).
- **Profile and notifications:** [Settings](../account/settings.md).
