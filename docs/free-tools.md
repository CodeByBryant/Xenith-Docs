# Free tools

Xenith has a set of free tools at [xenith.life/tools](https://xenith.life/tools). They
need no account and no email, and they work before you have signed up for anything.

## What they have in common

- **Everything stays on your device.** What you type is saved in your browser so you
  can come back to it, and it is never uploaded. Clearing your browser's site data, or
  pressing **Clear all** in a tool, removes it.
- **You take the result with you.** Each tool can copy its result as text and print it.
  Some also download a file: an image, a Markdown file or a calendar (`.ics`) file.
- **Analytics are counts only.** If you accept cookies, Xenith records which tool was
  used and how it was exported, plus a count such as how many tasks. It never records
  what you wrote, what you rated or how your energy felt. See
  [Privacy and data](./account/privacy-and-data.md).

## The tools

### Eisenhower Matrix Maker

Sort tasks by urgent and important. Add up to 50 tasks, then drag each into one of four
boxes (Do first, Schedule, Delegate, Let go), use the **Move to** menu on a task, or
press **Help me sort** to answer two questions about each one. Copy the result as text,
print it or download it as an image.

### Time Blocking Planner

Turn a task list into a schedule for the day. Set when your day starts and ends, add up
to 30 tasks with a length from 15 minutes to 8 hours, and place each one by picking a
start time, by picking a time on the timeline, or with **Fill my day**, which places
tasks in order in the earliest free gaps. Select a block to change its start or length;
blocks that overlap are outlined. Download the day as a calendar file for a date you
choose, copy it as text or print it.

### Morning Routine Builder

Add steps with a length in minutes, or start from a template, and see the clock time of
every step. Work forward from when you wake up, or back from when you need to leave to
find the latest time you can start. Add the routine to your calendar as one event or one
event per step, every day, on weekdays only or just once. The times stay the same
wherever you open the calendar.

### Weekly Review Generator

Four steps: look back (what went well, what did not get done and why, what drained you,
what gave you energy), rate each of the eight areas of life from 1 to 5 if you want to,
look ahead (up to three priorities, one thing to protect, one thing to let go of or
delay), then read the finished review. When you rate at least two areas it names the
highest and lowest from your own numbers. Copy it, download it as a Markdown file or
print it.

### Energy Audit

Rate your energy from 1 to 5 for each hour of a day, from 6 AM to 10 PM, and tag an hour
with what you were doing if you like. Log other days too. Once you have at least 8
entries from at least 3 different days, the audit shows your average by hour, your
highest and lowest two-hour windows, and how energy compared across activities that have
enough entries. Until then it tells you how much more it needs. **See an example**
previews made-up data without saving it.

### Work-Life Balance Quiz

Sixteen short statements, two for each of the eight [life dimensions](./life-dimensions/overview.md),
answered from 1 (not true) to 5 (very true). Each area gets a score out of 10, and the
result names your highest and lowest areas and shows a chart. It is a self-check, not a
diagnosis, and it is not compared with anyone else. You can open the result in the Wheel
of Life to adjust any score.

### Wheel of Life and Pomodoro Timer

The **Wheel of Life** lets you rate the eight areas of life from 1 to 10, see them as a
wheel, share a link and download an image. The **Pomodoro Timer** runs a 25-minute focus
and 5-minute break cycle with ambient sound, the same idea as [Focus](./daily-workflow/focus.md)
in the app.

## Bringing a result into Xenith

If you use the Eisenhower Matrix Maker, the Time Blocking Planner or the Weekly Review
Generator and then sign up, onboarding can offer your first task, first block or first
priority as your first intention.

- **It is an offer, not a transfer.** You see a small note with a **Use this** button.
  Nothing is filled in or saved until you press it, and you can ignore it.
- **It stays on your device.** The result waits in your browser for up to 14 days, is
  cleared when you finish onboarding, and is never sent to analytics.
- **Only the latest tool counts.** Starting over in a tool, or clearing its data, removes
  its offer.

## Using them with Xenith

The tools are standalone, and Xenith has a matching place for each result: your
[daily intentions](./daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md) for a sorted task list,
[Routines](./daily-workflow/routines.md) for a morning routine, the
[Reflection](./daily-workflow/reflection.md) page for a review, the
[daily check-in](./daily-workflow/daily-check-in.md) for energy, and
[Life Dimensions](./life-dimensions/overview.md) for your balance over time.
