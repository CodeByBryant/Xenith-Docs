# Graph

The **graph** draws your pages as dots and the [links](./links-and-backlinks.md) between
them as lines, so you can see which ideas are connected and which are on their own.

> **Free vs Pro**
>
> The graph around the page you have open is free. Showing **every page at once** is
> part of [Xenith Pro](https://xenith.life/app/pricing).

## Open it

In Projects, select the graph icon (**Link graph**) at the top, next to the sidebar
button. Select it again, or the **X**, to close it. Opening a page or database also
closes it.

## This page (free)

**This page** shows the open page, the pages it links to, and the pages that link to it.
Choose **1 step** for those neighbours, or **2 steps** to include their neighbours too.
The page you opened is drawn darker.

If the page has no links yet, the graph says so. Type [`[[`](./links-and-backlinks.md)
in a page to link it to another.

## All pages (Pro)

**All pages** shows every page you have, across all projects:

- **Search** narrows the picture to pages whose title contains what you type.
- **Project** shows one project's pages.
- **Hide pages with no links** leaves out pages nothing connects to.

It shows up to your **2,000 most recently edited pages**, and says when there are more.
On the free plan, choosing **All pages** shows a note that it is part of Pro.

## Reading the picture

- A bigger dot means more links.
- A line is drawn once per direction however many times a page links, or to however many
  headings of the other page.
- Only links between **pages** are drawn. Links to databases and to your intentions,
  habits, reflections and Focus sessions are not.
- The notes inside a database row are not part of the graph.
- Drag to move around and use the scroll wheel or the zoom buttons to zoom. With more
  than 40 pages an overview map appears in the corner.
- The layout is fixed once drawn: dots do not drift or wobble, and the same pages
  always look the same.
- Click a page to open it.

## Prefer a list?

Choose **List** instead of **Graph**. Each page appears with the pages it links to and the
pages that link to it, in words. The list works with a keyboard and a screen reader, and
choosing a page opens it.
