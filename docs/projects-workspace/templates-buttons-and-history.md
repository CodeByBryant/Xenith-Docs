# Templates, buttons and history

## Templates

A **template** is a page you keep to start new pages from.

- **Save a page as a template:** open the page, choose **Templates** at the bottom of the page list, give it a name and
  **Save template**.
- **Start a page from one:** **Templates**, then **Use** beside it. The new page opens in the project you are in. Its title is
  the template's name.
- **Edit a template:** **Edit** beside it lets you rename it, **Replace with the open page**, set or stop a repeat, pause it
  or delete it. You keep up to 100 templates.

Dates written as `@yesterday`, `@today` or `@tomorrow` when you saved the template are written again for the day a page is
made from it, in your time zone. A date you picked on the calendar stays that date. Block links and ids are not carried over:
each new page is its own.

### Pages on a schedule

Turn on **Make a page from it on a schedule** and choose how often: every N days, weeks (on the days you pick) or months, and
the project it goes in. A page is made at the **start of each matching day in your time zone**, starting today, titled with the
template's name and the day (for example "Weekly review · Mon, Nov 23").

- A day is made once. Running it again never makes a second page, and deleting the page does not bring it back.
- A day that went by is not made up later: the next page is made on the next matching day.
- **Pause** stops it without losing the template; **Resume** starts again from the next matching day.

## Buttons

Type `/button` for a button that does one thing when you press it:

- **Add an intention for today** with the title you set.
- **Start a Focus session** of the length you set (1 to 240 minutes). It opens Focus with the length filled in.
- **Log a habit for today**, once or a few times, for a habit you choose.

Nothing runs until you press the button. A button can **ask before it runs** if you turn that on. Choose **Edit** beside a button
(it shows when you hover it) to change it. Buttons can only do these three things; they never send anything outside Xenith. If a
habit is archived or deleted, the button says so and does nothing.

## History

The clock button at the top of a page opens its **history**.

- A version of the page is kept when you start editing a page that already has something in it, at most once every 10 minutes.
- **Free keeps the last 7 days** of earlier versions. **Pro keeps 90 days.** A version you **name** stays until you delete it
  (up to 20 per page).
- Choose a version to look at it. **Differences** lists the blocks that are in it but not in the page now, in the page but not in
  it, or different, in plain words.
- **Restore this version** puts the page's text and blocks back as they were. The page as it is now is kept in the history first,
  so you can come back to it. The title stays as it is.
- **Keep the page as it is now, named…** saves a named version.
