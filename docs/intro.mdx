# Welcome to Xenith

**Xenith helps you build a deliberate life — one dimension at a time.**

Instead of scattering your goals across a dozen apps, Xenith brings the areas
that matter into a single, calm workspace: your health, your mind, your work,
your relationships, and more. Each is a **Life Dimension** you can track,
reflect on, and grow.

This documentation walks you through everything — from creating your account to
mastering Focus sessions, AI growth paths, and your Insights dashboard.

## Who it's for

Xenith is for people who want to live intentionally — who'd rather make steady,
considered progress than chase noisy notifications and vanity metrics. There's
nothing here designed to make you feel guilty for missing a day. Just the things
*you* decide are worth your attention.

> **No streaks, by design**
>
> Xenith deliberately has **no streak counters**. Progress is about direction, not
> perfect attendance. You'll see your history and trends — never a number daring
> you not to break it.

## The core ideas

- **[Life Dimensions](./life-dimensions/overview.md)** — the eight areas of life
  you choose to track, each with its own color, tools, and rhythm.
- **[Daily Workflow](./daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md)** — set intentions in
  the morning, run [Focus](./daily-workflow/focus.md) sessions through the day,
  and close the loop with [reflection](./daily-workflow/reflection.md) at night.
- **[Growth & Insights](./growth-and-insights/growth.md)** — AI-generated growth
  paths, a personal coach, and analytics that turn your activity into
  understanding.

## Get started in three steps

1. **[Create an account](./getting-started/creating-an-account.md)** at
   [xenith.life](https://xenith.life) — free during beta, no credit card.
2. **[Complete onboarding](./getting-started/onboarding.md)** to pick the
   dimensions that matter to you right now.
3. **[Learn the interface](./getting-started/the-interface.md)**, then set your
   first intention and start a Focus session.

That's it — you're building deliberately.
