# Welcome to Xenith

**Xenith helps you build a deliberate life — one dimension at a time.**

Instead of scattering your goals across a dozen apps, Xenith brings the areas
that matter into a single, calm workspace: your health, your mind, your work,
your relationships, and more. Each is a **Life Dimension** you can track,
reflect on, and grow.

## Who it's for

Xenith is for people who want to live intentionally — who'd rather make steady,
considered progress than chase streaks and noisy notifications. There are no
vanity metrics here. Just the things you decide are worth your attention.

## Core ideas

- **[Life Dimensions](./life-dimensions)** — the areas of life you choose to
  track, each with its own color and rhythm.
- **[Focus](./focus)** — a distraction-free timer with ambient soundscapes for
  deep, intentional work.
- **Growth** — personalized paths that turn long-term goals into concrete steps.

## Get started

1. Create an account at [xenith.life](https://xenith.life).
2. Complete the short onboarding to pick the dimensions that matter to you.
3. Set your first intention and start a Focus session.

That's it — you're building deliberately.
