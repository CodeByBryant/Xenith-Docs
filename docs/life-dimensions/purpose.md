# Purpose

The **Purpose** dimension (shown in orange) is the compass for everything else.
When your daily actions are connected to what you actually value, the other
dimensions stop feeling like a checklist and start feeling like a life.

## Tools

### Values
Name the values that matter most to you and keep them somewhere you'll actually
revisit. Clear values are what make hard choices easier — they're the criteria
you measure options against when the answer isn't obvious.

### Decisions
A space to think through significant decisions with intention rather than
impulse. Lay out what you're deciding, weigh it against your values, and record
where you landed and why. Months later, that record is gold: you can see how a
choice played out and learn from your own reasoning.

## Why Purpose anchors the rest

The other eight dimensions describe *how* you live. Purpose asks *why*. A decision
log checked against explicit values is one of the most powerful self-knowledge
tools you can build — it turns scattered choices into a coherent direction.
