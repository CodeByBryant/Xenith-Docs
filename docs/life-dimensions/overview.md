# Life Dimensions

Life Dimensions are the heart of Xenith. Each dimension represents an area of
life worth attending to, with its own space, color, and set of tools. Together
they give you a balanced, at-a-glance picture of where you're thriving and where
something is quietly slipping.

## The eight dimensions

| Dimension | Color | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Health](./health.md) | Green | Biometrics, calories, water, workouts |
| [Mind](./mind.md) | Violet | Gratitude and thought audits |
| [Work](./work.md) | Blue | Energy & tasks, wins and losses |
| [Relationships](./relationships.md) | Pink | The connections you nurture |
| [Finances](./finances.md) | Amber | Transactions and money awareness |
| [Learning](./learning.md) | Cyan | Books and ideas |
| [Rest](./rest.md) | Indigo | Sleep and recharge |
| [Purpose](./purpose.md) | Orange | Values and decisions |

Each dimension keeps its color consistently throughout the app, so you always
know which area you're looking at.

> **Free vs Pro**
>
> The eight-dimension overview and every dimension's score are **free**, always.
> The tracking tool(s) inside each dimension (the actual logging screens linked
> from the table above) are part of [Xenith Pro](https://xenith.life/app/pricing).

> **Where does Focus fit?**
>
> [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) (deep, intentional work) is a core part of
> your day, but it's a standalone feature rather than one of the eight dimensions.
> You'll find it in the [Daily Workflow](../daily-workflow/focus.md).

## How tracking works

1. **Start from your goal.** During [onboarding](../getting-started/onboarding.md)
   you pick a goal, and each goal keeps three dimensions in view. Rate how each
   of the eight is going on the **Life Dimensions** page, and re-rate any time.
2. **Log what's meaningful.** Each dimension has dedicated tools, which are part
   of [Xenith Pro](https://xenith.life/app/pricing). Add entries at whatever
   cadence fits: daily, weekly, or only when something changes.
3. **Reflect.** Each dimension surfaces gentle insights so you can see patterns
   without being judged.

## Rating your dimensions

Open **Life Dimensions** in the app to see the eight areas as a wheel. Click a point
on the wheel to open that area, move its slider to a rough number from 1 to 10, and
let go: it saves when you do (from the keyboard, release an arrow key). Each week is
its own snapshot, so use the arrows at the top to look at earlier weeks and rate again
the next week to see how the shape moves. Earlier weeks are for looking, not editing.

**The first-visit walkthrough.** The first time you open the page, a short four-step
walkthrough points out the wheel, asks you to pick an area, asks you to rate it and
explains the weekly snapshots. It takes your real actions as the answer, so picking an
area or saving a rating moves it along, and nothing is skipped ahead of an explanation.
Close it any time with the close button or Escape. To see it again, choose
**Show me around**. Whether you have seen it is saved to your account, so it does not
come back on another device, in another browser, or after an update.

**Scores from the quiz.** If you took the
[Work-Life Balance Quiz](../free-tools.md) before signing up, the page can offer its
scores as a note with a **Use these scores** button. Nothing is saved until you press
it. It is only offered for the current week, and only while you have not rated anything
yet. If a result has no score for an area, that area is left unrated rather than
guessed.

## Opening a tool

Every tool is on the Life Dimensions page itself. Until you pick an area, the column
beside the wheel lists all of them, grouped under their dimension, and a click opens
one. Pick an area on the wheel and the same column shows that area's rating with its
own tools underneath. Tools that are part of Pro say so. From anywhere in the app, press
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on Mac) and type a tool's
name to go straight to it.

Finances and Learning each have one tool, so their old address
(**/app/dimensions/finances** and **/app/dimensions/learning**) opens that tool directly.
The other areas keep a page of their own that lists their tools.

## Dimension scores and balance

Each dimension reflects a **score** based on the recency and frequency of what
you've logged there. Log consistently and a dimension stays strong; neglect it
and the score gradually eases down. It's a quiet signal, not a scolding.

The [Insights dashboard](../growth-and-insights/insights.md) plots all your
dimensions together on a balance chart. One glance shows which areas are strong
and which need attention, without reading a report.

> **No streaks**
>
> Like everything in Xenith, dimensions show **trends and history**, never streaks.
> The aim is broad balance over time, not a perfect record in any single area.

## The framework behind it

Xenith's dimensions draw on the Wheel of Life coaching framework, positive
psychology research on wellbeing, and the idea that energy, not just time, needs
to be managed across every domain of a full life.
