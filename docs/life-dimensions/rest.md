# Rest

The **Rest** dimension (shown in indigo) treats recovery as part of the work, not
the absence of it. Sustained output depends on sustained rest, and this dimension
helps you give it the attention it deserves.

## Tools

### Sleep
Log your sleep so you can see the through-line between how you rest and how you
feel and perform. Even a simple record makes the connection between short nights
and rough days impossible to ignore.

### Recharge
Track the deliberate recovery activities that refill you — walks, downtime,
hobbies, time outdoors, whatever genuinely restores you. Recharge isn't wasted
time; it's what makes the focused time possible.

## Rest is not the opposite of productivity

It's easy to treat rest as what's left over. Xenith flips that: by giving Rest its
own dimension and score, recovery becomes something you do *on purpose* rather
than only when you've run out of road.

> **Note**
>
> Sleep lives here in **Rest**, not Health — a deliberate choice. It keeps recovery
> from being buried under fitness metrics and gives it the standing it deserves.
