# Habits

**Habits** is a daily checklist for the things you do, and for the things you'd like to
do less of. Open it from **Habits** in the menu. It is free for everyone.

It is built to state facts, not to keep score. There are no streaks, no
consecutive-day counts and no scores, and nothing changes colour or wording based on
how a day went.

## Two kinds of habit

Press **Add habit**, give it a name, and choose what it is:

- **Something I do.** You tick it when you've done it, for example "Walk after lunch".
- **Something I do less of.** You log it each time it happens, with **+** and **−**,
  for example "Coffee". You can add an optional **daily limit**.

A limit is shown next to your count as a plain fact: "2 today, limit 3". Xenith
doesn't compare the two, and the page looks and reads the same whether you are under
it, at it or over it.

## Any day

The checklist opens on today. Use the arrows above it to look at an earlier day and
log it there, or press **Back to today**. You can't open a day that hasn't happened yet.
Days follow your [timezone](../account/settings.md).

Under each habit you'll see a plain total for the last seven days, such as "4 days in
the last 7" or "10 in the last 7 days". It adds up what you logged. It doesn't look for
gaps or runs, and there is nothing to fall behind on.

If Xenith can't load a day's log (for example, you are offline), the habits show as
**Not loaded** and can't be changed until it loads, so a tap can't overwrite a count
you haven't seen. Press **Try again**.

## Edit, put away and delete

- **Edit** changes a habit's name or limit.
- **Put away** hides a habit from the checklist and keeps its history. Put away habits
  wait under **Put away**, and **Bring back** returns them.
- **Delete** removes the habit **and its history**. Xenith asks first.

## On the dashboard

The **Habits** card shows today's habits so you can tick or count them in a tap. It
starts hidden: add it from **Customize** on the dashboard (see [The
Interface](../getting-started/the-interface.md#arrange-your-dashboard)). If you don't
use Habits, you can hide the page and its card from
[Settings](../account/settings.md#pages).

## Your data

Habits and their history are included in [your export](../account/settings.md#export-your-data)
(the full JSON file) and are deleted with your account. Logging a habit counts as
activity on that day, like logging any other tracker.
