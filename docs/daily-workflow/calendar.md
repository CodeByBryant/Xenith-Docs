# Calendar

The **Calendar** (**/app/calendar**, under **Daily** in the sidebar) puts your plans and
what you did on one grid, so you can see a week or a month in context.

## What it shows

- Your [intentions](./daily-intentions.md), in the color of the Life Dimension each one
  belongs to.
- Your [Focus](./focus.md) sessions.
- The [routines](./routines.md) you completed, with how many steps you ticked.
- **Events**: the ones you add here, and the ones that sync in from Google Calendar or
  Notion (see [Calendar Sync](../integrations/calendar-sync.md)).

It shows what happened and what is planned. It doesn't grade a day.

## Views

Use the buttons at the top to switch between four views:

- **Month.** The whole month at a glance. Select a day to open it in the Day view.
- **Week.** Seven days side by side.
- **Day.** One day in detail: its intentions, routines and events.
- **Gantt.** A three-week window with one column per day, where an event that runs for
  several days is drawn as a bar across them. The arrows move it by a week.

**Today** brings you back to the current day, and the arrows step forward and back by a
month, a week or a day, depending on the view.

## Add an event

Press **Add event**, or double-click a day in the Month view, to open the event form:

- **Title**, and the **Date**.
- **Ends on** (optional). Fill it in for something that lasts several days, and it
  appears as a bar in the Gantt view. Leave it empty for a single-day event.
- **All day**, or a **Start** and **End** time.
- A **color**, and **Notes**.

Open an event to edit it, or to delete it.

## Events from Google Calendar and Notion

If you connected Google Calendar or Notion, their events appear here too, marked with
where they came from. Editing one of them changes it at the source as well ("edits
sync"). Press **Sync** to bring the latest events in now. If Google asks you to sign in
again, the button reads **Reconnect Google**. The details, and how to disconnect, are in
[Calendar Sync](../integrations/calendar-sync.md). To see your Xenith events in another
calendar app instead, see [Apple Calendar](../integrations/apple-calendar.md).

## Without a connection

The Calendar opens offline and shows what your device has saved. You can't add or change
an event until you're back online. See [Using Xenith offline](../getting-started/offline.md).
