# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Xenith free?

Yes — Xenith is **free to start**: no credit card and no trial countdown. Create
an account and use it as long as you like. See [Creating an Account](./getting-started/creating-an-account.md).

## What makes Xenith different from other productivity apps?

Most apps optimize for *activity* — more tasks, longer streaks, more
notifications. Xenith optimizes for *intention*. It brings the eight
[Life Dimensions](./life-dimensions/overview.md) that matter into one calm
workspace and helps you make steady, considered progress. There's no streak to
protect and no vanity metric to chase.

## Does Xenith have streaks?

No — by design. Xenith deliberately has **no streak counters** anywhere. You'll
see your history and trends, but never a number daring you not to break it.
Progress here is about direction, not perfect attendance.

## What platforms does Xenith run on?

Xenith is a web app that works in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. On a
phone you can **install it to your home screen** for an app-like experience,
including [push notifications](./account/notifications.md).

## How do I sign in?

With **email and a password**, or **Continue with Google** / **Continue with
Microsoft**. Existing accounts can also request a one-time **magic link**. See
[Creating an Account](./getting-started/creating-an-account.md).

## Is my data private? Do you train AI on it?

Your private entries are yours. Xenith does **not** sell your data and **never**
uses your private content to train AI models. AI features only send the specific
text you give them. Read the details in [Privacy & Data](./account/privacy-and-data.md).

## Which features use AI?

[Growth paths](./growth-and-insights/growth.md) (to generate your step-by-step
path) and [Coach](./growth-and-insights/coach.md) (your AI sounding board).
Everything else works without AI.

## Do I have to use all eight dimensions?

Not at all. Start with three or four that matter most, and add more later from
[Settings](./account/settings.md). It's better to be consistent in a few areas
than spread thin across all eight.

## Is Xenith for teams?

No — Xenith is built for **individuals**. [Projects](./growth-and-insights/projects.md)
is a personal workspace, not a team tool. There's no sharing or collaboration, and
that's intentional.

## Can I export my data?

Data export is on the roadmap. For account or data requests in the meantime,
follow the process in the [Privacy Policy](https://xenith.life/privacy).

## Where can I see what's new?

Check the [Changelog](./changelog.md) for the latest updates and improvements.

## Something's broken or missing. How do I get help?

Reach out through the support link in the app footer, or on the
[status page](https://status.xenith.life) if you suspect an outage.
