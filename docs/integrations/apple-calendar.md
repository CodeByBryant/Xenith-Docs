# Apple Calendar and other apps

Apple doesn't offer a sign-in that Xenith can use, so for Apple Calendar (and
any other calendar app that can follow a link) Xenith gives you a **private,
read-only link** instead.

> **Free**
>
> The calendar link is free on every plan.

## What it shows

- Your [intentions](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md), on the day they are
  planned.
- The events you created in Xenith.

It does not include events that came from Google Calendar or Notion (they are
already in those apps), and it never includes notes or reflections. It is
**read-only**: changes you make in your calendar app don't come back to Xenith.

## Getting your link

Go to **Settings → Integrations → Apple Calendar and other apps** (or the
**Connect your tools** step in onboarding) and choose **Show my link**. No link
exists until you ask for one.

- **On iPhone:** Settings, Calendar, Accounts, Add Account, Other, Add
  Subscribed Calendar, then paste the link.
- **On a Mac:** choose **Open in Apple Calendar**, or File, New Calendar
  Subscription, and paste the link.
- **Google Calendar, Outlook and others:** use their "add calendar from URL"
  option.

Your calendar app decides how often it refreshes; it can take a while for a new
intention to appear.

## Keeping it private

Anyone who has the link can see your intention and event titles and times. If it
reaches someone it shouldn't, choose **Make a new link**: the old one stops
working immediately, and you'll need to subscribe again with the new one.
