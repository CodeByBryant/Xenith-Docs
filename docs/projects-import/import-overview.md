# Import into Projects

**Import** copies content you already have into a [project](../growth-and-insights/projects.md).
Your originals are never changed: an import makes a copy in Xenith and nothing is
written back unless you later turn on [syncing for a Notion database](./sync-a-notion-database.md).

> **Free**
>
> Import is free on every plan, with the same limits for everyone (listed
> [below](#limits)). The free plan includes one project, so on that plan you
> import into the project you have.

## What you can import

| Source | What comes in | How you choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Notion](./import-from-notion.md) | Pages (with sub-pages) and databases, which become [Xenith databases](./databases.md) | Search what you have shared with Xenith |
| [Google Docs](./import-from-google-docs.md) | Documents, including their tabs, tables and images | Pick them in Google's own picker |
| [Word files](./import-word-files.md) | `.docx` files | Upload them |

## How an import works

1. In **Projects**, choose **Import** (in the sidebar, or on the empty screen).
   If it isn't there, importing isn't switched on for your account yet.
2. **Choose a source.** The first time, you connect the account. You can also
   connect Google Docs and Notion from the **Connect your tools** step in
   [onboarding](../getting-started/onboarding.md).
3. **Choose what to bring in.**
4. **Choose where it goes:** an existing project or a new one, inside a folder
   (named "Imported from Notion", "Imported from Google Docs" or "Imported from
   Word" unless you type another name).
5. **Review.** Xenith reads what you chose and tells you what it will import and
   what it won't carry over. **Nothing has been added to your projects yet.**
   Choose **Start import** to go ahead, or **Cancel import**.
6. **Progress.** Large imports take a few minutes because Notion and Google limit
   how fast they share content. Keep the tab open: if you close it, the import
   pauses where it is and continues when you come back (Projects shows a banner
   with **Continue**).
7. **Report.** A plain summary of what was added, what was approximated, what was
   left out and what to do about anything that failed. See
   [What carries over](./what-carries-over.md).

You can run one import at a time.

## Importing again

Importing the same thing again into the same folder is safe: it updates pages you
haven't edited and leaves your edits alone. See [Re-importing](./re-importing.md).

## Limits

These keep one import from overloading Xenith or the source. Anything over a limit
is left out and listed in the report.

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Pages in one import | 500 |
| Content blocks in one import | 25,000 |
| Databases in one import | 20 |
| Rows in one import | 10,000 |
| Pictures and files copied in one import | 250 MB in total, 25 MB each |
| Notion pages picked at once | 200 pages and 20 databases |
| Google Docs picked at once | 25 documents |
| Word files at once | 10 files, 25 MB each |

Across your account, you can have up to 50 databases, and one database holds up to
5,000 rows.

## Pictures and files

Images and files in imported pages are copied into Xenith's storage so they keep
working after the source link expires. They are stored under an unguessable
address: anyone who has the exact link can view the file. They are removed when you
delete your account; deleting a single page doesn't immediately delete its files.
See [Privacy and Data](../account/privacy-and-data.md).
