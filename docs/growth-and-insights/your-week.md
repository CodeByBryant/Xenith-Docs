# Your week

**Your week** is a short look back at one Monday to Sunday week, made only from your
own entries. It says what happened, and nothing else.

> **Being released gradually**
>
> Your week is rolling out in stages, so you may not see it yet. When it is on for
> you, it is the **This week** tab in [Insights](./insights.md), and it is linked from
> the **This week** card on your dashboard and from the weekly recap email. Older links
> to Your week take you to the same tab.

## What it shows

- How many of your planned [intentions](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md) you
  finished.
- Your [Focus](../daily-workflow/focus.md) time and how many sessions it was across.
- How many days you wrote a [reflection](../daily-workflow/reflection.md).
- The [Life Dimension](../life-dimensions/overview.md) where you finished the most
  intentions, but only once there are at least three and one clearly leads.
- A row of seven days with each day's Focus time, intentions and a dot for a day you
  reflected.

Days are your own days, in your timezone. A week with nothing in it says "Nothing
recorded for this week." It never shows a score, a target or a comparison with
another week, and it never frames a quiet week as a miss.

Use the arrows to look at earlier weeks, up to twelve weeks back.

## On this day

On the current week, **On this day** shows the start of any reflection you wrote on
this date a month ago and a year ago. It is shown only to you, in the app. That text
is never sent to analytics or put in an email.
