# Coach

**Coach** is your built-in AI sounding board — there when you want to think
something through, get unstuck, or turn a vague feeling into a clear next step.

## What Coach is good for

- **Thinking out loud.** Talk through a decision, a stuck project, or a goal
  that's lost momentum.
- **Turning reflection into action.** Bring a recurring theme from your
  [reflections](../daily-workflow/reflection.md) and get help shaping a concrete
  next move.
- **Planning a path.** Discuss a [Growth path](./growth.md) and how to adapt it to
  your week.
- **Gentle accountability.** A quick conversation to recommit to what matters,
  without pressure or guilt.

## How to use it

Open **Coach** from the sidebar (**/app/coach**) and start a conversation. Speak
plainly — Coach responds in context, oriented around the deliberate-living ideas
Xenith is built on: direction over speed, balance over burnout, intention over
autopilot.

## What Coach is not

> **Not a clinician**
>
> Coach is a reflective thinking partner, not a therapist, doctor, or financial
> advisor. For clinical mental health, medical, or professional financial matters,
> please consult a qualified professional. See also the
> [Mind dimension's note on therapy](../life-dimensions/mind.md).

## Privacy

Conversations with Coach are processed by an AI model to generate responses.
Xenith does **not** use your private content to train AI models. For the full
picture of how data flows, see [Privacy & Data](../account/privacy-and-data.md).
