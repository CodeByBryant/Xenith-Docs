# Worlds

Your **World** is a planet that starts as bare rock, like the moon, and changes
as you use Xenith. Its eight regions are your eight
[Life Dimensions](../life-dimensions/overview.md), so the part of the planet that
changes most is the part of your life you have been tending.

It is a quiet picture of what you have done, not a score to defend. Open it from
**Worlds** in the sidebar. The page fills the whole screen, with the planet in the
middle and a panel for its look on the right.

## It only grows

- A World never shrinks, fades or resets. If you delete old entries, your World
  keeps what it has.
- There is no daily target, no streak and no "you haven't" message. Xenith never
  sends you a notification about your World, and it never mentions a gap.
- Nothing is compared with other people. There are no shared scores.
- Everything about it is free. Customizing is cosmetic, and there is nothing to
  buy or unlock.

## What makes it grow

Growth is counted on Xenith's servers from your own entries, and your history from
the day you joined counts. Each local day (in your timezone) adds at most a fixed
amount, so a day of rapid ticking adds no more than a full day of doing.

| Activity | Adds | Counted per day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A finished intention | 3 | up to 5 |
| Focus time | 1 for every full 5 minutes | up to 60 minutes |
| A routine step | 1 | up to 5 |
| A reflection | 2 | 1 |
| A gratitude entry | 1 | 1 |

A finished intention or Focus session adds to the region of its
[Life Dimension](../life-dimensions/overview.md). Routines, reflections and
gratitude entries belong to no single dimension, so they are shared evenly across
all eight regions. After a Focus session, a line under the end of the session says
what it added, when it added something.

## The stages

Your World moves through seven stages: **Barren**, **Thin air**, **Oceans**,
**First life**, **Weather**, **Cities** and **Flourishing**. Your first finished
intention, or a ten minute Focus session, is enough to reach the second. Each region
also has its own look, from bare rock to lush, and the Worlds page lists all eight
regions with how far each has come (bare, lichen, green, lush or thriving) and the growth behind it.

The page describes the planet in words as well, so nothing depends on seeing
colours.

## Look around

Your World is a small 3D planet with real mountains, snowy peaks, shorelines,
forests, little towns that light up on the night side, clouds and animals.

- **Drag** to turn it, **scroll** or **pinch** to zoom, all the way down to ground
  level. The plus and minus buttons and the keyboard (`+`, `-`, arrow keys, `0` to
  go back out) do the same.
- **Point at a part of the planet** and a small card names its region and the
  numbers behind it, for example "lush · 22 from Mind activity".
- **Click or tap a region** to fly to it. It opens in the **Regions** tab of the
  panel, and **Back to the whole planet** takes you out again. Hovering a region in
  that list lights it up on the planet.
- Plants, towns and animals appear as a region grows and never go away. The same
  World always has the same trees and animals, and nothing appears at random.

## Customize it

The panel on the right has four tabs. On a phone it is a sheet that opens from the
**Customize** button.

- **Look:** give it a name (up to 30 characters), pick what it becomes
  (**Terra**, **Ocean**, **Desert**, **Ice** or **Ember**), **Roll a new planet** for
  a different landscape, change the colour of the oceans, greenery and atmosphere,
  choose how dramatic the mountains are, and turn the animals on or off.
- **Sky:** the stars, a nebula, rings, up to three moons, the lights on the night
  side, and whether it turns slowly or holds still. If your device asks for reduced
  motion, it holds still on its own.
- **Regions:** tint the regions with the Life Dimension colours, and see each
  region in words with the growth behind it.
- **About:** how your World grows.

Changes are previewed live and only saved when you press **Save changes**.
**Discard changes** puts your saved look back. If your device can't draw the planet,
a simple picture of it is shown instead.

## Turning Worlds off

Turn **Show Worlds** off in [Settings](../account/settings.md). The page, the
dashboard card and the menu entry all disappear, and your World is kept as it is, so
turning it back on later brings it back. You can also turn it off during
[onboarding](../getting-started/onboarding.md).

If you finished onboarding without a World, the dashboard offers to create one
once, and does not ask again.

## Your data

A World stores its name, its look and a growth score. The name is never sent to
analytics. It is part of your data export and is deleted with your account. See
[Privacy & Data](../account/privacy-and-data.md).
