# Growth Paths

Big goals fail for a simple reason: they stay abstract. Xenith's **Growth** turns
a long-term ambition into a concrete **path** of achievable steps, generated for
you, then yours to work through at your own pace.

> **Free and Pro**
>
> Free includes **one Growth Path** (one Life Dimension) and its **first two
> tiers**. [Xenith Pro](https://xenith.life/app/pricing) adds a path for each of
> the eight dimensions and opens every tier.

## How it works

1. Open **Growth** from the sidebar (**/app/growth**).
2. **Describe your goal** in your own words, like *"get comfortable speaking in
   public,"* *"learn to cook properly,"* *"ship a side project."*
3. Xenith uses AI to generate a **structured path**: an ordered series of nodes
   (steps), each a meaningful, doable milestone toward the goal.
4. Work through the path, marking nodes complete as you go. Your progress is saved
   and visible as a clear arc of development.

## What free includes

A path is generated in full, in five tiers. On the free plan the first two tiers
(**Entry** and **Foundation**) are readable and workable. The later tiers
(**Skill**, **Advanced** and **Mastery**) appear as blurred placeholders below a
dashed line, with a count of how many steps are waiting there. Their contents
are not sent to your browser until you upgrade; upgrading opens them without
regenerating anything.

To start a path in a different dimension on the free plan, **delete** the current
one first (you will be asked to confirm; its progress is cleared). A path can be
regenerated at most once every 7 days per dimension, and deleting a path does
not reset that wait.

## Turn a step into today's plan

On an unlocked step, choose **Plan for today** to add it to today's
[intentions](../daily-workflow/daily-intentions.md), tagged with the step's
dimension. Doing that does not mark the step complete; you mark steps yourself.

## Why a path, not a checklist

A flat to-do list treats every item as equal. A growth path is **sequenced**:
early nodes build the foundation later ones depend on. That ordering is exactly
what turns "I want to get better at X" into a route you can actually follow.

## AI you stay in control of

The AI proposes the path; you own it. The generated steps are a strong starting
structure, not a rigid prescription. Adapt your pace to your life. Xenith's AI
features are designed to assist your thinking, never to replace your judgment.

> **Your data and AI**
>
> The goal you describe is sent to an AI model to generate your path. Your private
> journal and reflection entries are **not** used for this, and nothing you write in
> Xenith is used to train AI models. See [Privacy & Data](../account/privacy-and-data.md).

## Growth and your dimensions

Growth pairs naturally with the [Learning](../life-dimensions/learning.md) and
[Purpose](../life-dimensions/purpose.md) dimensions: Learning for skills you're
building, Purpose for the why behind them. Progress on a path contributes to the
bigger picture you see in [Insights](./insights.md).
