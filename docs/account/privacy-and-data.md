# Privacy & Data

Xenith asks you to record some of the most personal things in your life — your
reflections, your gratitude, your decisions. That only works if you trust how it's
handled. Here's the plain-language version.

> **Authoritative policy**
>
> This page is a friendly summary. The legally binding documents are the
> [Privacy Policy](https://xenith.life/privacy) and
> [Terms of Service](https://xenith.life/terms) on the main site. If anything here
> differs from those, the official policy governs.

## Your private entries are yours

- Your journal, reflections, gratitude logs, and thought audits are **private to
  your account**.
- Xenith does **not** sell your data.
- Your private content is **never used to train AI models** — not ours, not anyone
  else's.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored securely with Xenith's infrastructure provider and is
protected by per-user access rules, so your records are only accessible to your
own authenticated account.

## How AI features use your data

Some features — [Growth paths](../growth-and-insights/growth.md) and
[Coach](../growth-and-insights/coach.md) — send the specific text you provide
(for example, the goal you describe or the message you send the coach) to an AI
provider to generate a response. Only the content needed for that feature is sent,
and it is **not** used to train models. Features that don't use AI never send your
data anywhere for processing.

## Error monitoring

To keep Xenith stable, the app reports technical errors to a monitoring service.
These reports are about *what broke*, not about the private content of your
entries.

## Notifications

If you enable [notifications](./notifications.md), Xenith stores the technical
subscription needed to deliver them. Disabling notifications removes that.

## Your controls

- **Hide dimensions** you no longer use without losing their data
  ([Settings](./settings.md)).
- **Disable AI features** simply by not using Growth or Coach.
- **Turn off notifications** any time.
- For account deletion or data requests, follow the process described in the
  [Privacy Policy](https://xenith.life/privacy).
