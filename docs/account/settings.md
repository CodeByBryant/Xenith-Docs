# Settings

Everything about how Xenith works for you lives in **Settings**
(**/app/settings**): your profile, appearance, notifications, connected tools and
more. The goal you pick in onboarding, and the dimensions it keeps in view, are
covered under Dimensions below.

## Profile

Update your **display name** and the basics of your account. How you sign in
(email and password, Google, or Microsoft) is covered in
[Creating an Account](../getting-started/creating-an-account.md).

## Dimensions

The three [Life Dimensions](../life-dimensions/overview.md) your goal keeps in
view are chosen in [onboarding](../getting-started/onboarding.md), and they
decide which tools your dashboard puts first. All eight dimensions stay available
under **Life Dimensions** in the sidebar, where you can re-rate any of them at
any time.

## Appearance

Switch between Xenith's **dark** (default) and **light** themes. The choice is
remembered per device, so you can run dark on your laptop and light on a bright
office monitor.

## Notifications

Enable or disable gentle reminders and manage push notifications. This has its own
guide: [Notifications](./notifications.md).

## Worlds

**Show Worlds** turns [Worlds](../growth-and-insights/worlds.md) on or off. Off hides
it everywhere (the page, the dashboard and the menu) and keeps your World exactly as
it is. **Customize your World** takes you to the Worlds page.

## Your data

From the privacy-related controls you can review how your information is handled.
For the full explanation, read [Privacy & Data](./privacy-and-data.md).

## Export your data

In Settings, choose **Export my data**, pick a format and press **Download**. Every
format is free.

- **Everything (JSON).** Every record Xenith holds for you, in one file. This is the
  complete backup.
- **Daily log (CSV).** One row for each day, for a spreadsheet or your own analysis.
  The columns are your energy check-in, mood (as a number from 1 to 5 and as a word),
  sleep hours and quality, caffeine, screen time before bed, exercise and stress,
  workouts, water, calories, Focus sessions, minutes, distractions and your average
  focus rating (`focus_quality_1to5`, from 1 to 5), intentions
  completed and gratitude items. Each supplement you have logged and each routine
  step gets its own column. A cell holds 1 for yes, 0 for something you recorded as
  no, and nothing at all when nothing was recorded, so you can tell a "no" from a
  gap. Days follow your timezone. It never includes text you wrote.
- **Journal (Markdown) and Journal (Word).** The same journal in two formats: your
  days by month, with mood, energy, sleep and the rest, what you completed, your
  gratitude and your reflections. A summary at the top gives counts and averages
  worked out from the entries below it. Days with rated Focus sessions show
  "rated X of 5".

The daily log and the journal only contain what you recorded. The sleep and
supplement trackers are Pro tools, so those columns are filled only if you used them.
You can start a few exports every few minutes. If an account is too large for the
daily log or the journal, Xenith says so and the full JSON export still works.

## Signing out

Sign out from the account menu. To sign back in, use your password, a magic link,
or your Google/Microsoft account, whichever you set up.
