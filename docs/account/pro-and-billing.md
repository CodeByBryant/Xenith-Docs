# Pro and billing

Xenith is free to use every day. **Xenith Pro** adds the extra tools listed in the
[FAQ](../faq.md). It is $7.99 a month, or $66.99 a year. Payments are handled by
Stripe, and Xenith never sees your card number.

## Your first week for $1

If you have never had a Pro subscription, you can try Pro for **$1 for 7 days**.

- **What you pay.** $1 today, when you start. Pro is on right away.
- **What happens after 7 days.** The monthly plan starts and your card is charged
  $7.99, unless you cancel first. Pro carries on without a gap.
- **Where to find it.** On the [pricing page](https://xenith.life/app/pricing), and in
  **Settings** under **Plan & billing**. The page shows the price you will pay after
  the week before you enter a card.
- **Who can take it.** Once per account, on the monthly plan. It is not offered on the
  annual plan, and it is not offered to an account that has had a Pro subscription
  before.

## Reminders before it renews

We email you **2 days** and **1 day** before the first full charge. Each email says the
date, the amount and the card that will be charged. If you are keeping Pro, there is
nothing to do. These are billing emails, so they are sent even if you have turned off
other email.

## Cancel any time

Open **Settings**, go to **Plan & billing** and choose **Manage billing**. Cancelling
during the week stops the renewal, and you keep Pro until the week ends. Cancelling
later stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you have
paid for. Your data is never deleted when Pro ends. Pro-only tools go back to showing a
preview.

## Receipts and questions

Stripe's receipt for each payment is emailed to you, and Settings shows your next
renewal date. For anything about a charge, write to
[support@xenith.life](mailto:support@xenith.life).
